About

I'm drawn to the parts of a project where raw information turns into a working system — cleaning messy data, testing what a model actually learns, and designing prompts that get AI to behave the way it should.

My work spans exploratory data analysis, model development, and the newer discipline of prompt engineering, with a steady interest in presenting findings clearly so they're easy to act on.

"Good data work is equal parts curiosity and discipline — asking the right question, then proving the answer."
